# Sum up 24 November 2026 world news in one image

Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty
`news-image-20261124` — $100, top 50 split.

## What it does

`main.py` builds a 1200×1600 news-summary infographic for Tuesday
24 November 2026: a header, a hero globe image, and the day's six top
stories, each with a headline and a one-line summary.

## Run

```sh
pip install pillow
python3 main.py   # writes output.png
```

## The six stories

1. Poland activates air defence as Russian drone enters its airspace
2. Trump says he brought Jensen Huang back to Nvidia
3. Ukraine reports fresh Russian strikes on Kyiv region
4. COP31 climate talks stall in Türkiye
5. Iran warns of countermeasures as US naval pressure mounts
6. Indonesia factory fire kills several, workers missing

Sources: major news wires, 24 November 2026.
